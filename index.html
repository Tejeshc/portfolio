About me.

      
Most network engineers pick up operations, certification testing, cloud-native infrastructure and R&D automation one piece at a time. I have done all four, in that order, and each one makes the next one sharper.

    

    

      

        
I am a System Validation Engineer at Netgear India's R&D router team in Bengaluru. I own IPv6 quality on the router product as QA lead, prove that L2/L3 protocols behave correctly on next-generation hardware, and build the automation, from Robot Framework and Python suites to a Docker harness that drives 300 VPN clients at once, that keeps proving it on every build.

        
My path started at IBUS, running enterprise infrastructure. At CNLABS I led IPv6 Ready Logo certification for five devices and ran interoperability testing across eight vendors. At InTeleCyber Services I validated Ericsson's Cloud Router in a live NFV environment on K3s and stood up VMware vCenter from nothing.

        
What that stack of experience gives me is end-to-end context: I know what a protocol looks like on the wire, in a conformance report, inside a container, and in a regression dashboard.
